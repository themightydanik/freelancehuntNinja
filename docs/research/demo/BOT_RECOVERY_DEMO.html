What a real service would verify

One agreed incident, client authorization, staging access, reproducible acceptance, a reversible change and a written handover. This demonstration is not a customer case, a security audit, cloud deployment evidence, or an SLA.

Rollback here returns to the pre-fix broken snapshot to prove reversibility; final recovery explicitly reapplies the fix. Local idempotency is not an exactly-once guarantee for external APIs.
