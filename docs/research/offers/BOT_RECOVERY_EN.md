# Draft service card — review only, not published

## Restore one Telegram bot or webhook workflow

A running bot stopped receiving updates, approval buttons stopped responding, or a prepared application needs a controlled deployment? Start with one clearly described incident and a reproducible acceptance check.

**Diagnostic: $75, up to 2 hours.** Review authorized staging configuration and redacted logs, reproduce the issue where possible, identify a supported cause or document the remaining uncertainty, and deliver a written repair/deployment plan. The diagnostic is a paid deliverable even if a safe repair needs a larger scope.

**Bounded repair: $150–250 total, quoted after diagnosis.** One agreed workflow, up to 6 hours including the agreed handover/regression support. The diagnostic payment is credited toward the total: a $220 total after a $75 diagnostic leaves $145. These are experimental draft prices, not validated transaction prices.

### Deliverables

- A reproducible before/after acceptance check on approved staging or synthetic fixtures.
- A minimal agreed correction, an exact rollback point and a short operating handover.
- A result report showing what passed, what remains unresolved and the limits of the verification.

Provide a description of the last known working behavior, the current symptom and authorized access through your own secure mechanism. Secret values stay outside reports and chat. Production changes and any real test messages require explicit client approval.

Acceptance is agreed before repair. A 7-day regression window covers the same repaired fault only, with up to 1 hour included within the 6-hour scope. New integrations, broad SaaS rewrites, security incident response, financial/custodial bot logic, 24/7 support and deleted personal-account/data recovery require separate scope or are excluded. Infrastructure and provider fees are separate and disclosed before approval.
